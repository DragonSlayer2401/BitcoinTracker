# Testing

Run `pnpm test` for Jest with Testing Library, `pnpm run format:check` for Prettier, and `pnpm build` for production compilation. Feature tests live in `src/features/BitcoinTracker/__tests__/`.

Tests cover market API validation and route failures, probability behavior and data-quality gates, immutable forecast observations, scheduled starts, persistence compatibility and failures, and user-facing target/timing flows. Stream tests additionally cover maker/aggressor conversion, trade-ID and timestamp continuity, duplicate/conflicting matches, heartbeat confirmation, reconnect resets, rolling flow, order-book replacement sizes and validation, depth changes, and strict deadline outcomes. Pressure model tests cover flow-dependent probabilities, bounded adjustments, uncertainty, and baseline fallback. New-policy integration tests verify weak/balanced publication and preserve legacy consensus/risk behavior. Research tests cover original input snapshots, withheld-window outcomes, export records, and storage behavior. Use deterministic timestamps and prices; unit tests must not make live exchange requests. A passing model test validates its implementation, not its predictive accuracy.

For browser acceptance, run the app with network access and verify:

1. A fresh Coinbase price, completed history, source attribution, and working ECharts 30m/1h/2h chart controls. Verify stream connecting/warming/live states, and that flow windows remain unavailable until complete 15/60/180-second history exists. Healthy REST price/history may support a clearly labeled pressure-free baseline estimate while flow warms or is unavailable, including new-policy fixed publication after its observation period. REST must never verify deadline settlement. Hover or tap a historical point to inspect its exact timestamp, price, and source; verify keyboard point inspection. Future-range tooltips must say model estimate, not observed price. Check the React Select schedule menu with mouse and keyboard, including focus, Escape, disabled state, and placement near viewport edges.
2. Editing the USD target changes the live preview before and during a forecast; “Use current” selects current price but need not force 50/50 when directional pressure is active. Verify that clearing/typing still works during active and scheduled forecasts, that different fixed/preview targets are labeled, and that recorded targets/probabilities/deadlines never change. After completion, New forecast retains the edited target.
3. **Schedule by → Start now** saves the target and begins observation with a deadline 15 minutes later. The prominent countdown starts at `15:00`, never `15:01`, then decreases. Fixed shows observation progress and no probabilities until at least three minutes. The first valid new-policy estimate can then publish without a 65% minimum or additional same-direction minute: verify a weak Above/Below **Slight lean** and an exact balanced 50/50 snapshot. It uses remaining time and then stays immutable. Reload keeps the original three-minute point/cutoff/deadline and requires fresh current inputs; unavailable pressure may fall back to baseline without fabricating old trade buckets.
4. **End time** derives the window start exactly 15 minutes earlier. At 12:18, choosing 12:30 displays a 12:15 window start and a `12:00` countdown; submitting begins observation now and preserves the 12:30 deadline. The earliest possible fixed call is 12:21. Empty, invalid, or non-future ends are rejected. The latest allowed end is 24 hours 15 minutes ahead. Joining with less than four minutes remaining produces **No clear signal** immediately because a complete observation period plus one minute of lead will not fit; Live remains available.
5. An end more than 15 minutes away schedules its future start. **Start time** still accepts a future local start within 24 hours. Saving a future schedule records target and timing independently of later preview edits without capturing original probabilities early; the separate live preview still covers 15 minutes from now. The main countdown stays at `15:00` while **Starts in** decreases. Reload retains the same window, and canceling before its start permits a new schedule.
6. At the scheduled start, the main countdown begins decreasing toward the fixed end. Fresh data begins observation within 15 seconds; starting 5 seconds late shows `14:55` remaining. Observation uses the saved target independently of live preview edits. The fixed call is issued only after its publication rule is met; the endpoint never shifts.
7. Missing or stale data through the capture grace window, or resuming a suspended tab after it, marks a future schedule's start as missed without creating or automatically recovering a forecast. The missed schedule can be dismissed. Joining a window already in progress requires a new explicit submission with fresh data. Directly selected past starts and future starts more than 24 hours ahead cannot be scheduled.
8. Missing or stale essential quote/history data pauses recording and removes live probability values; reconnect restores live estimates. An already recorded original prediction remains visible through feed errors. At the deadline the live estimate stops. For a strict-outcome entry, resolve only the last complete-stream match at/before the deadline, at most five seconds old, with continuity including that match and a heartbeat strictly past the deadline. A later quote alone, a missing match, a stale last trade, or a stream starting after expiry must not resolve it. Without eligible confirmation it becomes unobserved. Original predictions remain visible and offer **New forecast** to begin again explicitly. Reload preserves results. Automated tests cover timing boundaries and delayed delivery without a 15-minute test wait.
9. Existing valid version 1–4 history loads into version 5 without changing earlier calls, schedules, model/policy versions, or settlement semantics. New entries retain `trade-pressure-log-return-v1`, `pressure-snapshot-v3`, `coinbase-last-trade-at-deadline-v1`, and verified trade/continuity metadata. Older saved v1/v2 observation policies must still use their 65%/60-second consensus rules and, for v2, market-risk vetoes. History distinguishes old sampled and verified outcomes. Observation metadata and no-call decisions persist. Storage failures display a warning instead of implying a successful save.
10. A selected or running end 12 minutes away limits the **Live model 80% range** to those 12 minutes, with an **End** chart label and matching **Live window volatility**. Both reflect the separate live estimate, not the fixed original prediction. A future window more than 15 minutes away keeps the 15-minute preview. Invalid or expired horizons remove the forward interval and endpoint claim; 30m/1h/2h history controls still work.
11. The panel layout at 1366×768 and 1440×900 desktop sizes, including end-time selection, joined-window, scheduled-start and active-countdown states, without page scrolling. At 390px/320px mobile widths, confirm a prominent readable countdown, natural vertical scrolling, and no horizontal page overflow. Check keyboard access and visible focus for target, scheduling options, date/time input, buttons, and history/model dialogs; closing a dialog restores focus and preserves the current setup.
12. For a new pressure snapshot, reverse the direction shortly before minute three: it should publish the current valid estimate at the original earliest time, without accumulating a further minute. Missing/stale essential data must delay publication only until it recovers or the unchanged cutoff: five minutes after observation starts or one minute before the end. At that cutoff unavailable data produces a permanent no-call; returning data must not issue it retrospectively. Separately verify saved v1/v2 consensus still requires 60 seconds, seven distinct increasing trades, and no gap above 15 seconds. No-call entries have no issued probabilities, correctness, or Brier contribution. Coverage includes weak/balanced issued snapshots and withheld decisions, while excluding ongoing observation and older immediate calls.
13. Inspect current momentum/acceleration, relative volume, range/volatility, spread, target proximity, 15/60/180-second executed flow, large-trade activity, and 5/10/25-basis-point depth. Distinguish executed volume from displayed liquidity. Missing/warming inputs stay unavailable rather than zero. With identical spot/target/history, valid buying versus selling pressure should alter probabilities through the model; responsive movement and spread should alter uncertainty. Opposing flow, missing book data, spread ambiguity, depth loss, or ordinary volatility/jumps within the essential validity limits must not independently veto new snapshots. Verify that missing pressure produces a labeled baseline fallback. Prior v2 windows must retain their original veto behavior. No view should claim a calibrated success rate for this experimental adjustment.
14. **Research data → Export research JSONL** exports versioned observation, decision, and outcome rows with original target/deadline, capture/input timestamps, features, flow, liquidity, probability, and outcome definition. Observations sample every five seconds during analysis. Decision rows preserve actual decision inputs; a restored decision with unavailable capture evidence must leave those inputs unavailable. Withheld windows remain tracked separately through their deadline, including after starting another forecast or clearing visible journal history; they retain no issued probability/correctness. Unobserved outcomes and reasons remain exportable for coverage analysis. Storage-cap/failure warnings retain earlier rows rather than silently replacing them. This browser-local export is not an immutable central journal.

New journal outcomes represent the last verified trade at/before the deadline under the explicit five-second age and continuous-heartbeat rule. Older entries retain their sampled price within 15 seconds after a deadline. Scheduled capture and prospective research recording require this browser to be running; no server-side background timer exists. A live scheduled-start check and a live end-to-end 15-minute observation are separate from fast automated tests; report which were actually run. Do not claim that candle backtests verify stream completeness or that unit tests demonstrate predictive accuracy.

The fitted candle-study candidates are never imported into the production tracker. Event/news, cross-venue, funding/open-interest, and liquidation feeds are future candidates and are not present in this implementation.
