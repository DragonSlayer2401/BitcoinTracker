# Testing

Run `pnpm test` for Jest with Testing Library, `pnpm run format:check` for Prettier, and `pnpm build` for production compilation. Feature tests live in `src/features/BitcoinTracker/__tests__/`.

Tests cover market API validation and route failures, probability behavior and data-quality gates, immutable forecast observations, scheduled starts, persistence compatibility and failures, and user-facing target/timing flows. Use deterministic timestamps and prices; unit tests must not make live exchange requests. A passing model test validates its implementation, not its predictive accuracy.

For browser acceptance, run the app with network access and verify:

1. A fresh Coinbase price, completed history, source attribution, and working ECharts 30m/1h/2h chart controls. Hover or tap a historical point to inspect its exact timestamp, price, and source; verify keyboard point inspection. Future-range tooltips must say model estimate, not observed price. Check the React Select schedule menu with mouse and keyboard, including focus, Escape, disabled state, and placement near viewport edges.
2. Editing the USD target changes the live preview before and during a forecast; “Use current” centers it near 50/50. Verify that clearing/typing still works during active and scheduled forecasts, that different fixed/preview targets are labeled, and that recorded targets/probabilities/deadlines never change. After completion, New forecast retains the edited target.
3. **Schedule by → Start now** saves the target and begins observation with a deadline 15 minutes later. The prominent countdown starts at `15:00`, never `15:01`, then decreases. Fixed shows **Observing market**, an observation countdown, and no probabilities. Live keeps updating. After at least three minutes, a full minute of consistent qualifying fresh trades can issue the fixed call; it uses the remaining horizon and then stays immutable. Reload restores the target and deadline but must observe a new minute of quotes before publication.
4. **End time** derives the window start exactly 15 minutes earlier. At 12:18, choosing 12:30 displays a 12:15 window start and a `12:00` countdown; submitting begins observation now and preserves the 12:30 deadline. The earliest possible fixed call is 12:21. Empty, invalid, or non-future ends are rejected. The latest allowed end is 24 hours 15 minutes ahead. Joining with less than four minutes remaining produces **No clear signal** immediately because a complete observation period plus one minute of lead will not fit; Live remains available.
5. An end more than 15 minutes away schedules its future start. **Start time** still accepts a future local start within 24 hours. Saving a future schedule records target and timing independently of later preview edits without capturing original probabilities early; the separate live preview still covers 15 minutes from now. The main countdown stays at `15:00` while **Starts in** decreases. Reload retains the same window, and canceling before its start permits a new schedule.
6. At the scheduled start, the main countdown begins decreasing toward the fixed end. Fresh data begins observation within 15 seconds; starting 5 seconds late shows `14:55` remaining. Observation uses the saved target independently of live preview edits. The fixed call is issued only after its publication rule is met; the endpoint never shifts.
7. Missing or stale data through the capture grace window, or resuming a suspended tab after it, marks a future schedule's start as missed without creating or automatically recovering a forecast. The missed schedule can be dismissed. Joining a window already in progress requires a new explicit submission with fresh data. Directly selected past starts and future starts more than 24 hours ahead cannot be scheduled.
8. Missing or stale data pauses immediate recording and removes live probability values; reconnect restores live estimates. An already recorded original prediction remains visible through feed errors. At the deadline the live estimate stops. A timely eligible trade resolves an active forecast; a missed outcome window is recorded as unobserved. Both retain the original prediction and offer **New forecast** to begin again explicitly. Reload preserves that result. Automated tests cover timing boundaries and delayed delivery without a 15-minute test wait.
9. Existing valid version 1 and 2 history loads into version 3 without changing earlier calls. Observation metadata and no-call decisions persist. Storage failures display a warning instead of implying a successful save.
10. A selected or running end 12 minutes away limits the **Live model 80% range** to those 12 minutes, with an **End** chart label and matching **Live window volatility**. Both reflect the separate live estimate, not the fixed original prediction. A future window more than 15 minutes away keeps the 15-minute preview. Invalid or expired horizons remove the forward interval and endpoint claim; 30m/1h/2h history controls still work.
11. The panel layout at 1366×768 and 1440×900 desktop sizes, including end-time selection, joined-window, scheduled-start and active-countdown states, without page scrolling. At 390px/320px mobile widths, confirm a prominent readable countdown, natural vertical scrolling, and no horizontal page overflow. Check keyboard access and visible focus for target, scheduling options, date/time input, buttons, and history/model dialogs; closing a dialog restores focus and preserves the current setup.
12. Before publication, reverse the direction, repeat the same quote, regress its timestamp, fail a request, or provide stale history: these must never manufacture confirmation. A qualifying run spans 60 seconds, contains at least seven distinct exchange trades, and has no gap exceeding 15 seconds. At five minutes (or one minute before the end, whichever is earlier), an unqualified signal becomes a permanent no-call; returning strong data must not issue it retrospectively. No-call entries have no probabilities, result correctness, or Brier contribution. Coverage counts issued calls versus completed observation decisions and excludes legacy calls and ongoing analysis.

The journal represents sampled prices within 15 seconds after a deadline. Scheduled capture requires this browser to be running; no server-side background timer exists. A live scheduled-start check and a live end-to-end 15-minute observation are separate from fast automated tests; report which were actually run.

`node scripts/evaluate-forecasts.mjs --days=14 --end=2026-09-08T00:00:00Z` runs the separate historical evaluation. Add `--offline` to replay validated cached candles without network access. See `docs/forecast-evaluation.md` for chronological splits, coverage, target-distance effects, and why this minute-candle proxy cannot validate the exact live quote policy. Historical predictive evaluation is separate from deterministic correctness tests.
